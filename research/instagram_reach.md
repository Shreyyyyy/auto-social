### 1. Official Instagram Reels Ranking Signals
* **Top 3 Core Ranking Metrics:** Instagram head Adam Mosseri stated that the three primary ranking signals across Reels are **Watch Time** (total seconds watched including loops/replays, not raw 3-second views), **Sends per Reach** (DM shares), and **Likes per Reach**. *(Source: Adam Mosseri / Meta, Jan 2025)*
* **Connected vs. Unconnected Reach Priority:** Mosseri highlighted that **Sends per Reach** (DMs) is the dominant factor for *unconnected reach* (expanding to non-followers on Explore/Reels tabs), carrying significantly more weight than likes. **Likes per Reach** primarily influences distribution among existing followers (*connected reach*). *(Source: Adam Mosseri via Instagram Creator series, Jan–Feb 2025)*
* **Raw Views Deprioritized:** Instagram officially confirmed that raw headline view counts are no longer the primary indicator of Reel success; algorithms actively evaluate **Share Rate**, **Skip Rate**, and completion retention. *(Source: Meta / Adam Mosseri update, May 2026)*
* **Originality Ranking:** The recommendation engine directly favors original creators over reposters/aggregators. When identical videos are found, Instagram dynamically replaces the reposted version in recommendations with the original creator’s upload. *(Source: Instagram Creators Announcement, Apr 2024 / confirmed through 2025–2026)*

### 2. Instagram SEO (Captions, Audio, Text, & Hashtags)
* **Captions & Keywords:** Meta indexes text context natively. Place descriptive keywords in the first 1–2 lines of captions (e.g., specific Hinglish/English concepts like *"Class 10 CBSE Math Tips"* rather than vague titles). *(Source: Instagram Creators Guide, 2025)*
* **On-Screen Text & Video OCR:** Instagram’s AI scans visible on-screen text overlays to classify the Reel’s topic and determine topical search relevance. *(Source: Meta / Adam Mosseri Creator Education, late 2025–2026)*
* **Spoken Audio Transcription:** The algorithm transcribes spoken dialogue in Reels to categorize topics and feed the search engine. Clearly speaking educational keywords in Hindi/Hinglish improves recommendation accuracy. *(Source: Meta Creator Guidelines, 2025)*
* **Alt Text:** Descriptive alt text (accessible via *Advanced Settings*) helps Instagram’s computer-vision models index what is visually displayed on screen. *(Source: Meta Accessibility & Discovery Documentation)*
* **Hashtags Guidance:** Adam Mosseri confirmed that *"hashtags do not increase reach"* and act strictly as organizational categorization labels. Instagram tested capping hashtags down to **3–5 highly relevant niche tags** per post (deprecating broad tags like `#reels` or `#viral`). *(Source: Adam Mosseri / Meta Platform Updates, late 2025)*
* **Profile Name & Bio Keywords:** The "Name" field (distinct from the @handle) is fully searchable. Indian educators should include primary search keywords here (e.g., *"Rahul Sharma | UPSC & History Mentor"*). *(Source: Instagram Search & Discovery Documentation)*

### 3. Best Posting Days & Times for India (IST)
* **Peak Weekday Windows (IST):** Data benchmarks analyzing audience behavior in India show highest scroll volumes occur during late afternoon commutes and post-dinner wind-downs:
  * **Primary Window:** **7:00 PM – 9:30 PM IST** (Weekdays, with Tuesday and Wednesday showing the highest engagement spikes).
  * **Secondary Window:** **12:00 PM – 2:00 PM IST** (Lunch break catch-up).
  *(Sources: Sprout Social Global & Regional Engagement Benchmarks, Apr 2026; Buffer 9.6M Post Study, Oct 2026)*
* **Weekend Behavior:** Saturdays and Sundays skew toward midday: **11:00 AM – 1:00 PM IST** and late evenings **8:00 PM – 10:00 PM IST**.
* **Meta’s Stance on Timing:** Adam Mosseri noted that while recency matters in the first few hours of a post, there is no single universal "golden hour" for all accounts; creators should anchor their schedule to their account's specific **"Most Active Times"** in *Professional Dashboard > Insights*. *(Source: Meta Creators, 2025–2026)*

### 4. Features & Tactics: What Officially Helps
* **Trial Reels:** Launched globally to let creators test Reels with **non-followers only** before displaying them on their profile grid or followers' feeds. It provides performance metrics after 24 hours (views, sends, likes) and allows manual or automated sharing to your followers if it performs well within 72 hours. Ideal for testing new Hinglish educational hooks without fatiguing existing followers. *(Source: Meta Official Announcement, Dec 2024 / Updated 2025)*
* **Collab Posts:** Allows up to 5 collaborators on a single Reel; shares the same view count, comments, and sends pool across both audiences, directly increasing total distribution and mutual discoverability. *(Source: Meta Creators Documentation)*
* **Broadcast Channels:** Delivers one-to-many push notifications directly into followers’ DM inboxes. Excellent for education creators to announce new Reel drops, driving immediate velocity and watch-time signals. *(Source: Meta Product Announcement)*
* **Stories Reshares:** Mosseri clarified that Stories and Reels use separate algorithms. Resharing your Reel to Stories drives "Connected Reach" (existing followers) and early watch time, but it does *not* automatically boost the Reel within the public Reels recommendation algorithm. *(Source: Adam Mosseri Q&A, 2025)*
* **Pinned Comments:** Does not trigger algorithmic reach on its own, but functions as a conversion/clarity tool to keep a pinned question, Hinglish summary, or CTA at the top of the comment section to drive viewer comments. *(Source: Instagram Creators)*
* **Replying to Comments:** Early comment velocity is a positive interaction signal, but Mosseri reiterated that meaningful comments and conversations hold value, while generic short replies are secondary to DM shares (sends). Replying within the first hour encourages longer dwell times on the post. *(Source: Meta Transparency Center & Mosseri Q&A)*

### 5. Cover / Thumbnail Grid Best Practices
* **Reels Canvas Size:** Full-screen vertical **9:16 aspect ratio** (1080 × 1920 px).
* **Feed Preview Crop:** Appears as **4:5 aspect ratio** (1080 × 1350 px) in standard home feeds.
* **Profile Grid Crop:** Most profile grids display as **1:1 square** (1080 × 1080 px) or **3:4 vertical rectangle** (1080 × 1440 px).
* **Safe Zone Rule:** Keep all educational titles, headings, and faces within the **center 1080 × 1080 px zone**.
* **UI Obstruction Margins:** Avoid placing critical text in the **top 220 px** (account handles/status bars) and **bottom 430–450 px** (caption overlay, audio title, CTA buttons), as well as the **far-right edge** (like, share, and comment rails). *(Source: Meta Ads & Creator Design Guides, 2025–2026)*

### 6. What Gets Reels Down-Ranked or Suppressed
* **Third-Party Watermarks & Logos:** Reels containing visible watermarks (e.g., TikTok, CapCut watermarks) are explicitly deprioritized and excluded from Explore/Reels recommendations. *(Source: Adam Mosseri & Meta Recommendation Guidelines)*
* **Aggregator & Unoriginal Content:** Instagram penalizes serial aggregator accounts that repost unoriginal media without adding material value (new voiceover, commentary, or transformative editing). Original versions are algorithmically substituted into recommendations instead. *(Source: Meta "Helping Creators Find New Audiences" update, Apr 2024 / enforced 2025–2026)*
* **Low Video/Audio Quality:** Videos that are blurry, low-resolution (below 1080p), heavily compressed, or uploaded with distorted audio get restricted from recommendations. *(Source: Meta Recommendation Guidelines)*
* **Duration Limits for Discovery:** While Reels can be longer, Meta recommends keeping Reels under **90 seconds to 3 minutes** for discovery eligibility; videos exceeding 3 minutes lose standard Reels recommendation distribution. *(Source: Instagram Creators)*
* **Engagement Bait:** Explicitly telling users to spam-comment single letters or using manipulative engagement bait can flag the account in the *Account Status* dashboard and limit recommendations. *(Source: Meta Recommendation Guidelines & Transparency Centre)*
